Lane order: Outer → Inner → Middle → Outer → Middle → Inner → Outer → Middle. Untimed strides advances one stride per button press; arrows or lane buttons select the lane.
- **Target Football**: Score 5 target goals in the displayed order. Aim inside the highlighted ring: the crosshair must be within 25 SVG units of the target centre. Target order: upper left → lower right → upper centre → lower left → upper right. Misses may be retried without losing completed goals. Tap/drag or use sliders/arrow keys to aim.
